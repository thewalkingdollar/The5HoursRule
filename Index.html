The Core Concept

What is the 5 Hour Rule?

The 5 Hours Rule of Attack is a performance protocol: process and act on critical information from the previous day within the first 5 hours of your current day.

Information decays. Insights fade. Opportunities close. By attacking within 5 hours, you convert yesterday's intel into today's momentum—before email, meetings, and noise hijack your focus.

This isn’t about doing more. It’s about doing what matters, first.

The 5 Hour Window

6:00 AM - 11:00 AM

Peak cognitive hours. Zero distractions. Pure execution.

After 11:00 AM

Decision fatigue sets in. Reactive mode begins.

The Playbook

How to Apply It

Four steps. Every morning. No exceptions.

1

Review Yesterday's Intel

First 15 minutes: scan notes, emails, meetings, and insights from yesterday. Capture anything actionable.

Keep a “Parking Lot” doc for all intel
2

Prioritize Ruthlessly

Rank by impact: What moves revenue? What unblocks others? What expires soon? Pick your top 3 only.

Impact > Urgency > Easy wins
3

Execute Before Distractions

No email. No Slack. No social. Phone on airplane mode. Deep work on priority #1 until complete.

Defend your first 5 hours like a vault
4

Log & Reset

End of hour 5: document what you shipped. Capture new intel for tomorrow. Clear the deck.

Compound daily: 3 actions x 250 days = 750 wins
